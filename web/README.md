
# Formatting

Teams are listed by
Club's initials-AgeGroup/Gender-Head Coach's Last Name


For example, WSC-G8B-Smith
Coaches with two teams in the same Age/Gender group
will have "1" or "2" appended to the team name
E.g., WSC-G3B-Jones1 & WSC-G3B-Jones2

## Game Lengths

Game Lengths
Level 	#Players/Side 	Game Length
G2 	4v4 (No Goalkeepers) 	40 Minutes (4 10-Minute Quarters)
Play 10 minutes, take 1 minute break
Play another 10 minutes, take 5 minute break (Halftime)
Play a third 10 minutes, take 1 minute break
Play fourth and final 10 minutes of the game
G3, G4 	7v7 	50 Minutes (2 25-minute halves, 5 minute halftime)
G5, G6 	9v9 	60 Minutes (2 30-minute halves, 5  minute halftime)

High School 	11 v 11 	80 Minutes (2 40-minute halves, 5 minute halftime)
